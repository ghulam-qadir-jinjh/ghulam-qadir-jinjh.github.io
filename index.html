Ghulam Qadir Jinjh

I am an electrical engineer and MSc student in Digital Electrical Energy at the Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (Skoltech), Moscow, graduating in June 2027. My research focuses on power electronics for electric vehicles, in particular bidirectional AC-DC converters for Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) and Grid-to-Vehicle (G2V) applications.

My work covers PLECS modelling, phase-shift control, DSP-based hardware implementation on the TI TMS320F28379D, and fault location in overhead transmission lines.
