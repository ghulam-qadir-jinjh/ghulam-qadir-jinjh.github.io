About

I am an electrical engineer and MSc student in Digital Electrical Energy at the Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (Skoltech), graduating in June 2027. My work focuses on power electronics for electric-vehicle applications. My thesis addresses a single-stage isolated bidirectional AC-DC Dual Active Bridge (DAB) converter for Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) and Grid-to-Vehicle (G2V) systems, covering PLECS modelling, phase-shift control and DSP-based hardware implementation.
