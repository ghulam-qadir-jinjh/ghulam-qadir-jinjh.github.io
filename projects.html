Single-Stage Isolated Bidirectional AC-DC DAB Converter for V2G/G2V

MSc thesis | Skoltech, 2025-26

Designed a 5 kW converter with 12 MOSFETs (8 back-to-back MOSFETs on the AC side, full H-bridge on the DC side; 230 V rms AC, 200 V DC, 20 kHz). Developed a variable-duty phase-shift controller (PI with feedforward) and a hybrid CC/CV/TPS controller. In PLECS the converter reaches up to 99% efficiency, THD below 2.1% and near-unity power factor over the full range. Hardware implementation on a TI TMS320F28379D DSP is in progress.
